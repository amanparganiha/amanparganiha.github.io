Computer vision has seen remarkable progress in 2024. Let's explore the key trends shaping the field.

## Foundation Models

Models like SAM (Segment Anything) and DINOv2 have changed how we approach CV tasks. Instead of training from scratch, we can now leverage these foundation models.

## Edge Deployment

With optimized architectures like EfficientNet and MobileNet, deploying CV models on edge devices is more practical than ever.

## Multimodal Learning

The convergence of vision and language (CLIP, GPT-4V) is opening new possibilities for understanding visual content.

## What's Next?

- Video understanding at scale
- 3D scene reconstruction
- Embodied AI and robotics
