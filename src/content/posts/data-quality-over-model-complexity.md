After years of building ML systems, I've learned one lesson that stands above all others: data quality matters more than model complexity.

## The 80/20 Rule of ML

80% of your model's performance comes from your data. The remaining 20% comes from architecture and hyperparameter choices.

## Practical Steps

### 1. Data Profiling

Understand your data distributions, missing values, and outliers before training.

### 2. Labeling Quality

Invest in clear labeling guidelines and inter-annotator agreement metrics.

### 3. Data Augmentation

Use smart augmentation strategies that reflect real-world variation.

## Conclusion

Before reaching for the latest transformer architecture, invest in understanding and cleaning your data. The ROI is almost always higher.
